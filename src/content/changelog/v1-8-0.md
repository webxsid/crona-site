Crona v1.8.0 adds a clearer separation between reviewing the day and changing the workspace, while making scheduled day boundaries first-class daemon behavior.

## Added

- Start of Day and End of Day schedules, configured independently in Settings.
- Local-time weekday overrides for either schedule, with durable occurrence deduplication.
- A dedicated read-only `Summary` view in the TUI and matching `crona summary` CLI snapshots for days and ranges.
- Summary periods for calendar week, calendar month, yesterday, rolling last-X-days, and custom date ranges.
- Summary exports as Markdown, PDF, or clipboard Markdown.

## Changed

- Workspace views now appear before export tooling in the sidebar and view-jump menu. Summary uses `[g]`; History keeps `[y]`.
- Repository, stream, and issue-rollup exports ask for their target and prefill it from the active context when available.
- Hard-limit timer alerts can expose Commit and Extend actions to native alert subscribers.

## Operational notes

- Day-boundary schedules run in the daemon's configured local timezone and only fire while the daemon is running.
- Start of Day is enabled at `00:00` by default; End of Day is disabled until configured.
