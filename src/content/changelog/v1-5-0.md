Crona v1.5.0 sharpens the terminal experience with a clearer Rollup focus chart, a snapshot-first Wellbeing layout, and a streamlined Momentum surface for custom momentum management.

Checkout selection now behaves more like a real selector, modal dialogs no longer distort the active view, and the surrounding TUI plumbing has been simplified for maintainability.

The website release story also uses updated Rollup and Momentum screenshots to show the current surfaces more accurately.
