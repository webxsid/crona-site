Crona treats habits as a first-class part of the daily workflow. They are not folded into issues, and they are not just a note-taking feature. A habit is a recurring routine that you can schedule, complete, review, and compare over time.

This page covers the operational side of habits: how Crona schedules them, how completion is recorded, how habit history works, and how custom streak periods keep different routines readable.

## How Habits Fit The Day

Habits appear in the Daily view alongside issues, but they stay separate from issue state. That separation matters because a recurring routine has a different shape from a one-off task.

In practice, Crona uses habits for things like:

- daily routines you want to keep visible
- weekday-only or weekly routines
- recurring actions that should not become issues
- repeatable work you want to track without turning it into a task list

## Habit Schedules

Crona supports three schedule types:

- `daily`
- `weekdays`
- `weekly`

Those schedules let you express different habits without forcing every routine into the same cadence. A daily habit behaves differently from a weekday-only routine, and Crona keeps that distinction explicit.

## Completing Habits

Habit completion is recorded per day. That means you can mark a habit complete, remove that completion later if needed, and keep the day’s record honest.

That behavior is useful when:

- you complete a routine later in the day
- you need to correct an accidental completion
- you want the record to reflect what actually happened instead of just what was intended

## Habit History

Habit History is the long-view surface for habits.

Instead of only seeing whether a habit is due today, you can review the completion record over time. That makes it easier to notice whether a routine is actually sticking, slipping, or being completed in a way that matches the way you use Crona.

Habit History also helps when you want to compare habits against each other or check whether a streak is meaningful rather than accidental.

## Custom Streak Periods

Not every habit should use the same streak logic. Crona now supports custom habit streak periods so the streak model can match the routine itself.

The available periods are:

- `day`
- `week`
- `month`

That gives you room to define streaks in a way that fits the habit. A daily routine can stay daily, while a broader routine can track progress over a weekly or monthly window instead of pretending every habit should be measured the same way.

## How Habits Show Up In Daily

The Daily view shows habits that are due on the selected date. That makes habits part of the day’s working surface rather than a separate maintenance area.

If you are planning the day, the habit pane helps you answer:

- what routines need attention today?
- which habits are due in this window?
- how do routines and issues sit together in the same day?

## How Habits Relate To Wellbeing

Habit history and streaks feed into the wider reflection loop as well. They help explain not just what you did, but how consistently you have been keeping the routines that support the rest of your work.
