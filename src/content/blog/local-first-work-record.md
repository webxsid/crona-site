Most work tools ask you to translate the day into someone else's system. Crona starts with a smaller question: what actually happened?

That record should be available without an account, a network connection, or a dashboard designed for someone else. Issues, focus sessions, habits, and check-ins live together on your device so the context stays close to the work.

## Local is a product decision

Keeping the record local is not only a privacy posture. It changes the shape of the product. There is less ceremony between noticing something and writing it down. The app can be useful before it knows anything about you.

It also makes the archive durable. A workday remains a file you can inspect, export, and carry forward rather than a row in a service you have to keep paying attention to.

## Readability is the point

The useful view is rarely a single metric. It is the relationship between an issue you opened, the session you spent on it, and the note you left when you stopped.

Crona is built around that relationship: capture the day quickly, then make enough space to understand it later.
